I am a final-year PhD candidate at the University of Würzburg, advised by Prof. Carlo D'Eramo. I study how neural networks learn, especially in reinforcement learning, and how to turn that understanding into better training methods. Recently, I have been working on [memory and plasticity](https://openreview.net/forum?id=Kgz0zlJVS6) in continual learning and on [how learning dynamics differ between reinforcement and supervised learning]({{ '/papers/ric/' | relative_url }}).

Earlier in my PhD, I worked on [policy search]({{ '/papers/abs/' | relative_url }}) and [policy optimization](https://rlj.cs.umass.edu/2026/papers/Paper8.html). Before that, I interned in the Scool team at Inria Lille and studied at ENS Paris-Saclay (MVA) and Télécom Paris.

I will graduate in spring 2027 and am on the job market for research positions. I am always happy to chat, so feel free to reach out.
