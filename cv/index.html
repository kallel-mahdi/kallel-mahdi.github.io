Publications

  • 2026
    The Spectral Anatomy of Plasticity Loss
    AXIOM Workshop at NeurIPS 2026

    Uses random matrix theory to explain loss of plasticity. Over a stream of tasks, the directions that hold earlier knowledge absorb a growing share of each new gradient. Making them read-only keeps the network trainable without losing what it learned.

  • 2026
    Do Not Imitate, Reinforce: Iterative Classification via Belief Refinement
    Transactions on Machine Learning Research (TMLR), 2026

    Trains classifiers with reinforcement learning instead of imitating labels with cross-entropy: a recurrent agent refines its prediction step by step and is rewarded for each improvement. It achieves a better balance of accuracy and calibration than supervised training, and stays calibrated under human label noise, where cross-entropy grows overconfident.

  • 2026
    PPO+: Enhancing Proximal Policy Optimization
    Reinforcement Learning Journal (RLC 2026)

    Shows that PPO is held back by its critic. Training the critic on off-policy data, while keeping the policy update on-policy, substantially improves PPO and narrows the gap to off-policy algorithms, without implementation tricks.

  • 2026
    Use the Online Network If You Can: Towards Fast and Stable Reinforcement Learning
    ICLR 2026
  • 2024
    Augmented Bayesian Policy Search
    ICLR 2024

    Makes Bayesian policy search aware that it is solving an MDP: the performance difference lemma turns a learned critic into the prior over returns. The method outperforms earlier Bayesian optimization approaches, with the largest gains on high-dimensional policies.

    Cyclicity-Regularized Coordination Graphs
    RLC 2024
